## 🐰 How Should I Print My BuniBuni?

To achieve a clean, durable, and visually appealing BuniBuni body, we recommend the following setup:

---

### 🧱 Infill & Pattern
- **Infill Density**: 20%
- **Infill Pattern**: Gyroid  
  This combo offers excellent strength-to-weight ratio and smooth internal surfaces—ideal for organic shapes like a bunny with mechanical details.

### 🎨 Filament Choice
- **Recommended**: PLA  
  PLA is easy to print, supported by virtually every FDM printer, and provides a crisp finish—perfect for showcasing mechanical features.
- **Alternatives**:
  - **PETG**: Adds durability and slight flexibility.
  - **ABS**: Offers heat resistance and supports post-processing (e.g., acetone smoothing).
  - **Silk PLA or Matte PLA**: Great for aesthetic finishes depending on your bunny’s vibe.

### ⚙️ Print Settings
- **Layer Height**: 0.2 mm for a solid balance between detail and speed.
- **Print Speed**: 50–60 mm/s to maintain quality.
- **Wall Thickness**: 1.2 mm or more for structural integrity.
- **Supports**: Enable if your BuniBuni body and legs have overhangs—mine definitely did.
- **Brim or Raft**: Use a brim for better bed adhesion, especially if your BuniBuni body is compact (mine was *slightly* so).

### 🌡️ Temperature Settings
- **Nozzle Temp**: 200–210°C for PLA (adjust based on filament brand).
- **Bed Temp**: 60°C for PLA.
- **Cooling**: Enable part cooling fan for sharper details and improved overhang performance.

### 🧼 Post-Processing (Optional)
- **Sanding**: Light sanding for smoother surfaces.
- **Painting**: Use acrylic paints to highlight mechanical details or add personality.
- **Assembly**: If your bunny includes moving joints or electronics, print parts separately and assemble post-print.
